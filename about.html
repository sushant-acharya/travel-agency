About Himalayan Horizon

Founded in Kathmandu in 2012, Himalayan Horizon Travels is a government-licensed trekking and tour agency dedicated to safe, sustainable, and unforgettable adventures across Nepal. Our team of certified local Sherpa guides brings deep cultural knowledge and mountain expertise to every itinerary.

Whether you are standing beneath Mount Everest, spotting one-horned rhinos in Chitwan, or exploring ancient temples in the Kathmandu Valley, we handle permits, logistics, and eco-friendly accommodations so you can travel worry-free.
